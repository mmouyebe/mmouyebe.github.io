HyperDynSim

HyperDynSim is a computational framework for simulation, analysis, and inference of higher-order dynamical systems on hypergraphs.

The project is organized around three main modules corresponding to the research pillars of the program: dynamics, inference, and control. It supports the study of pattern formation, structure inference, and intervention in complex systems with higher-order interactions.

PNT-Lunar

PNT-Lunar is a MATLAB-based research codebase for orbit determination and state estimation in cislunar navigation problems.

The project focuses on trajectory estimation, Kalman filtering, and dynamical modeling in the context of autonomous systems and control.
