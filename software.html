HyperDynSim

HyperDynSim is a computational framework for simulation, analysis, and inference of higher-order dynamical systems on hypergraphs.

The project is organized around three interconnected components corresponding to the core pillars of my research program: dynamics, inference, and control. Its long-term goal is to provide a unified computational environment for studying pattern formation, structure reconstruction, and intervention in complex systems with higher-order interactions.

The framework is currently under active development, with ongoing efforts focused on dynamical systems modeling, hypergraph reconstruction, and control of emergent behavior.

PNT-Lunar

PNT-Lunar is a MATLAB-based research codebase for orbit determination and state estimation in cislunar navigation problems.

The project focuses on trajectory estimation, Kalman filtering, and dynamical modeling for autonomous spacecraft navigation in the Earth–Moon system.

Research code currently maintained in a private repository.
