Research

I develop mathematical frameworks for understanding, inferring, and controlling higher-order dynamical systems.

My research studies how higher-order interactions shape dynamics in complex systems, how latent interaction structure can be reconstructed from data, and how such systems can be influenced through principled interventions. My work lies at the intersection of nonlinear dynamics, network science, inverse problems, and control theory.
