About

I develop mathematical and computational frameworks for understanding, inferring, and controlling higher-order dynamical systems.

My research lies at the intersection of nonlinear dynamics, network science, inverse problems, and control theory. I am particularly interested in how higher-order interactions shape collective behavior, how latent interaction structure can be reconstructed from data, and how such systems can be influenced through principled interventions.

Current themes: higher-order dynamics, hypergraph reconstruction, and control of emergent behavior.
